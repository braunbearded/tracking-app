Zaelio is an offline Android app for creating custom trackers, recording sessions, and storing measurements locally in SQLite.

Features:

* Create custom trackers with number, text, and timer fields
* Record and continue sessions
* Sort trackers and sessions locally
* Import and export trackers, sessions, or full JSON backups
* Light/dark theme, accent color, font size, and field size settings
* No Google Play Services, Firebase, ads, tracking, cloud sync, or account required
